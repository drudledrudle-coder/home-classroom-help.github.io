        How it works

        A scheduled bot scrapes three sources every 15 minutes: the Pokémon Center UK storefront backend (watched-SKU availability), X communities (live "dropped / restock" chatter), and isthereadroptoday.com (reference status + historical log). A transparent cadence model combines a history base rate (day-of-week + how overdue we are) with a live signal boost to produce today's probability and forecast the next drop. No accounts, no carts — read-only signals only.

        This is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to The Pokémon Company, Pokémon Center, or isthereadroptoday.com. Predictions are estimates, not guarantees.
